The ride so far

I’m Nepali, born and raised in Arunachal Pradesh, and I still build from here. I care most about ownership: if I ship it, it’s mine when it breaks too. Beyond that, I don’t stress too much.

  1. 2020

    A weather app, alone in a hostel room

    Covid had emptied the hostel, so I sat down and built a weather app. It was my first project, and it’s why this page shows Itanagar’s weather.

  2. 2021

    NIT Arunachal Pradesh, then CleverX

    Finished my B.Tech in computer science and joined a B2B user-research startup as one of its two engineers. Built the design system, led the React 18 migration, moved search from Algolia to Typesense, built the screener engine and took over transactional email.

  3. Side roads

    Meypato and My Health Ajin

    Meypato, a rental booking app. My Health Ajin, which never made it to market. Not every road goes through.

  4. 2024

    Senior Software Engineer

    Took the platform’s AI and real-time work end to end: the study-builder agent, AI voice interviews, the Twilio to LiveKit migration, a public API, billing and multi-seat teams. Since then, email consent, invite delivery at scale and a lot of legacy code retired.

  5. 2026

    Two things of my own

    Codebase Archaeology, which explains why code exists from its git history, and Year Progress Wallpaper, which puts the year’s progress on your lock screen.

  6. Now

    Still riding

    Owning the AI layer at CleverX, playing football, and taking the bike out whenever the weather allows.
