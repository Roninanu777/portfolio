LLM Study-Builder Agent

An agent (Anthropic and OpenAI, Langfuse-traced) that authors whole research studies: tasks, screeners, skip logic.

Its tool-call dependency scheduler orders every call by the reads and writes each tool declares, so a study never references a screener that doesn't exist yet.

AI Voice Interviews

Interviews run on LiveKit agents with a persona and voice driven by the study config, live transcription through AssemblyAI and Cartesia, participant reconnection, and server-side session completion.

LLM Cost Control

Audited Anthropic usage across the platform and traced a cost spike to low-cache-hit LLM-as-a-judge eval rules.

Request sampling and model selection fixed it: roughly 16% off the monthly LLM bill.
